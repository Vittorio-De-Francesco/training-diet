Double progression

Stay in the rep range. When every working set reaches the top of the range with clean form and about 1-2 reps in reserve, add the smallest available load next time and restart near the bottom. Example, pulldown at 6-10: 65 kg × 6/6/6 → 10/10/10 over several weeks → 67.5 kg × 6/6/6.

Deload

Check around weeks 6-8. Deload earlier if several lifts stall for about 2 weeks, joints stay achy, sleep worsens or swims feel disproportionately hard. Same exercises, about half the working sets, no grinding reps, swims at about 70% of normal distance.

Swim technique
  • Look down and exhale steadily underwater.
  • Rotate through the torso to breathe; don't lift the head.
  • Controlled reach, catch and pull. No paddles or pull buoy at first.
  • If wrist tension returns, shorten the session and don't force the catch.
  • Start at the bottom of each target (about 1,000 / 800 / 600-700 m) and increase one session at a time.
Calories

Estimated maintenance about 2,500 kcal/day (range 2,350-2,650), with training adding about 200-215 kcal/day on average. The nutrition plan's 2,200-2,300 kcal is a moderate deficit of about 200-300 kcal. Calibrate with 2-3 weeks of weekly average weight.
